Mentorship

Ask someone who has already done it. WeCan Connect pairs continuing students with alumni who can give advice and guidance on courses, internships, careers, and life after graduation.

  • I'm a student: find an alumni mentor
  • I'm an alumnus: offer my time and advice

How mentorship works

  1. Sign up. Tell us who you are and what you want to talk about.
  2. Get matched. We match you by field of study, interests, and availability.
  3. Meet. Chat online or in person. Most pairs meet once or twice a month.
  4. Grow. Set goals together and check your progress each term.

Common questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Mentorship on WeCan Connect is free for students and alumni.

How much time will it take?

Most pairs meet for about an hour, once or twice a month. You agree the schedule together.

Can I change my mentor?

Yes. If the match isn't working, contact us and we'll find someone else.

Who can become a mentor?

Any graduate who wants to share their experience. We'll check your alumni status before you're listed.
