About WeCan Connect

More Than a Community. A Place to Grow.

WeCan Connect is a community platform designed to bring together students, alumni and mentors within the WeCan Academy network.

Inspired by the learning and collaboration encouraged at WeCan Academy, the platform creates opportunities for members to continue learning, share knowledge, collaborate on projects and support one another beyond the classroom.

Whether you are beginning your technology journey or already building your career, WeCan Connect provides a space where you can learn, contribute and grow.
